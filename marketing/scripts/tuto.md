# Le tuto Babi Life (depuis zéro)

A real tutorial, filmed in the real game on a phone's screen (`tuto.mjs`), a finger on each tap, each step numbered and titled at the top, Awa's words under it, her voice by ElevenLabs (Lea, `tuto-voix.mjs`). Seven episodes of about 30–40 s, then the full cut.

| Ép. | What | Lines (`tuto-voix.mjs`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Commencer | 1 sign up on lifeinbabi.com · 2 create the perso · 3 birth, the family drawn by lot | `e1-*` |
| 2 · Tu grandis | bébé (the bottle, coucou-caché with maman), enfant (school: raise your hand, answer the maître), ado (the first phone, Gbairai), adulte at 18 (the Boutique: grow up at once) | `g-*` |
| 3 · Ta vie de tous les jours | 4 the needs' gauges, the joystick and « Courir », a garba at the maquis, « Dormir » at home | `e2-*` |
| 4 · Le boulot et l'argent | 5 the Boulot app, DJ de quartier (no diploma), the shift at the club (21h–2h: film it then), the pay in Waye | `e3-*` |
| 5 · Les jeux | 6 foot à cinq, billard, kart, the street fight, the 8 américain in the phone | `e4-*` |
| 6 · Les amis | 7 who's online (« Tout Babi »), greet, add as a friend, invite, the Tikitoko live | `e5-*` |
| 7 · Voyager et réussir | 8 the plane, Bassam's beach, the N'Zi safari · 9 « devenir debout », lifeinbabi.com, the QR code | `e6-*` |

`EP=n node tuto.mjs` makes episode n (`out/babi-life-tuto-ep<n>-1080x1920.mp4`); `node tuto-complet.mjs` puts the seven together (`out/babi-life-tuto-complet-1080x1920.mp4`, 2 min 56: the first hook, each episode from its first step, the last end card). All seven made on 7 November 2026.
